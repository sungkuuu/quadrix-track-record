# qETH1.5X P19 deposit cap — events so far: faults of our testnet vault contracts and of their keys

Measured: 2026-10-20, about 02:55 UTC. Data range: 2026-08-21 (first workflow run of this repository) to
2026-10-20 02:45 UTC (GIWA Sepolia block 39119211). Sources, all public: the published decisions
(`trackrecord/decisions/`, 30 files, and `trackrecord/decisions.jsonl`), this repository's commit history
(`origin/main` at `0567882`), its GitHub Actions runs (558 runs; the 17 that did not succeed were read from their
logs), and the chain (GIWA Sepolia, chainId 91342).

## Result

**Events counted: 0.**

The P19 row of the decision says the cap limits the loss from an event — a fault of the vault contract or of a key
that marks or runs it, not a price path — and gives the number of such events so far. On the criterion below, none
has happened on any of our testnet vaults. The 17 workflow runs that did not succeed and three reverted calls are
listed below, and none of them is counted: in each the vault kept the last value it held, or nothing reached a vault.

## What is counted

An event is counted when, on one of our testnet vaults on GIWA Sepolia (the qX20 tracking vault, the basket
vaults, the qBTC2X mark vault, and the retired qDV and QBV v3), one of these happened:

1. the vault contract did something its code was not meant to do (a wrong price accepted, assets moved, shares minted
   or burned against its rules);
2. a key with a role on the vault (owner, keeper, deployer, guardian) was exposed, lost, or used by anyone other than
   its holder;
3. a transaction signed by such a key put a wrong value on chain — a price, a holding or a parameter that was not the
   one intended.

Not counted, and listed when found:

- a transaction that reverted, a send the node refused, or a run that stopped, when the vault kept its last value.
  A vault that is not marked keeps its last price; that is the state the contract and the rulebooks expect when a mark
  is missing;
- failures off chain (record commits, anchoring of documents, data sources, alerts);
- corrections of published documents and records that changed no vault;
- findings of code reviews: a finding is not an event.

## Limits

- The count rests on what was recorded publicly: a fault that left no failed run, no commit and no decision would not
  be found here. Every vault write is on chain; the failures above were found from the run logs, not from a scan of
  every transaction of every vault.
- Rehearsal and test findings on forks are not events on the testnet and are not listed.
- Zero events over the testnet vaults' life so far, with no deposits held by the two vaults measured in
  `p19-usual-cost-2026-10-20.md`, is a small sample; it is not a rate.
