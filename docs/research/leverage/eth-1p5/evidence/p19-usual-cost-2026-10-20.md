# qETH1.5X P19 deposit cap — usual cost: the largest deposits of the two vaults with the same cap

Measured: 2026-10-20, about 02:50 UTC, at the pinned block **39119211** of GIWA Sepolia (chainId 91342; block time
2026-10-20 02:45:27 UTC). Data range: every event of each vault from its creation block to the pinned block.
Sources: the chain (`eth_call` at the pinned block and at each creation block, https://sepolia-rpc.giwa.io) and the
GIWA Sepolia explorer API (https://sepolia-explorer.giwa.io/api — `logs/getLogs` for events, `account/txlist` for
transactions). Vaults: the qX20 tracking vault `0x2A165501ddA6e430fF98E82682f53CA8465Bb21f` (created block 36015792,
2026-09-14) and the qBTC2X mark vault `0x7C10C33aEd202207Bb0C054B32C0708ABAC54070` (created block 38422951,
2026-10-12). Amounts are test dollars (MockUSD, 6 decimals).

The usual cost of a 1,000,000 test-dollar cap, measured on these two vaults, is **zero: no deposit was ever held, so
the cap has never turned anyone away.** The measurement says nothing about demand, because neither vault has ever
been open to deposits below its cap:

- **qX20 tracking vault.** Created 2026-09-14 04:41:48 UTC with a cap of 1,000,000 test dollars; deposits paused
  26 minutes later, at 05:08:09 UTC (`setDepositsPaused(true)`, block 36017373, tx
  `0xdcc2cff0e6127c21496ad6ef5370f91eba3d16917cb66bface6d6abe25d1d57f`) and never unpaused. Of 129 transactions sent to
  it, 124 are marks, 1 is the pause, 1 is the creation, and 3 reverted: on 2026-09-26 a deposit of 0, a redemption of
  0 and a deposit of 5 test dollars (blocks 37113252, 37113254, 37113265) — all while deposits were paused.
- **qBTC2X mark vault.** Created 2026-10-12 with a cap of 0, as its rulebook requires (deposit cap 0 until opened).
  No `DepositCapUpdated` event since: the cap of 1,000,000 test dollars named in its rulebook (P19) and inception decision
  has not been set on chain as of the pinned block. Its 7 transactions are the creation and 6 marks.

So the qETH1.5X decision's phrase "the same cap as the qBTC2X mark vault and the qX20 tracking vault" holds for the
qX20 tracking vault on chain; for the qBTC2X mark vault it holds for the rulebook value, not for the chain value (0).

## Method

The contract's cap check is `totalAssets() + assets <= depositCap`, with `totalAssets() = totalSupply() ×
navPerShare ÷ 1e6` (`QuadrixIndexVault`). The script starts from the state read at the creation block (the
constructor sets the cap without an event), replays every log of the vault in order — share mints and burns
(`Transfer` from or to the zero address), `NavUpdated`, `DepositCapUpdated`, `DepositsPaused`, `Deposit`, `Withdraw` —
recomputes `totalAssets()` after each, and keeps the largest value and its ratio to the cap then in force. The
replayed end state (supply, price, cap, `totalAssets`) is compared with `eth_call` reads at the pinned block: equal
for both vaults. As a second check, every transaction sent to each vault is tallied by function (explorer `txlist`).
No transaction is sent and no key is used.
